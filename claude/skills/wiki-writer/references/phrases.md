# Vocabulary tells

A density map, not a ban list. One of these words proves nothing; three in a paragraph means the text was generated and not reread. Legitimate technical uses exist for most of the second and third tiers, so judge by whether the word is carrying meaning or filling space.

The measurements behind this list come from Kobak et al. (Science Advances, 2025), who tracked excess vocabulary across 15.1 million PubMed abstracts: "delve" appeared at roughly 25 times its pre-2023 rate, "underscores" and "showcasing" around 9 times.

## Tier 1: rewrite on sight

These almost never survive contact with a concrete sentence. Rewrite them on sight.

delve, delving · tapestry · a testament to · in the realm of · navigating the complexities · harness the power of · myriad of · plethora of · paradigm shift · game-changer · synergy · unlock the potential · at the end of the day · in today's fast-paced world

## Tier 2: suspicious in clusters

Fine alone in a precise sentence, damning together.

robust · comprehensive · seamless · cutting-edge · innovative · streamline · empower · foster · enhance · elevate · pivotal · intricate · cornerstone · groundbreaking · transformative · unprecedented · meticulous · leverage (as a verb meaning "use") · utilize · facilitate · underscore · multifaceted · holistic · spearhead · embark · endeavor

## Tier 3: light signals, do not purge

Normal English that shows up more often in generated text. Notice them, do not hunt them.

crucial · essential · vital · significant · furthermore · moreover · additionally · consequently · nevertheless · ultimately · notably · arguably

## Substitutions

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| utilize | use |
| leverage (verb) | use |
| facilitate | help, or name the actual mechanism |
| robust | name the property: fault-tolerant, well-tested, handles retries |
| comprehensive | drop it, or say what is covered |
| seamless | drop it |
| in order to | to |
| a number of | the number, or "several" |
| is able to | can |
| it is important to note that | drop the whole clause and state the fact |

## Russian

Every page here is English, so Cyrillic in `content/` is a lint error rather than a style problem. Keep this list for chat and drafts that get translated in: канцелярит («является», «данный», «осуществляется», «в целях»), filler signposting («стоит отметить», «важно понимать», «таким образом»), openers («в современном мире»), and the calqued «Это не просто X, это Y».
